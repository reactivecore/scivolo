# ADR 0018 — REPL completion: dotty's labels, never more

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-10-09

## Context
dotty's REPL completes on Tab with the labels `Completion.completions` computes on the typed
input; which names it offers depends on the JDK it runs on. Our universe knows the Scala
library and javalib from TASTy, not the JDK, and our typer gives up on constructs it does not
support. Research: [11](../research/11-completion.md).

## Decision
- **Parity:** the candidates are the distinct labels dotty's REPL offers for the same session
  and input, on JDK 21. Never a label dotty does not offer; a label it offers may be missing
  when our universe cannot know it, and each such miss is listed in the corpus (`miss>`).
  Where the set is uncertain, nothing is offered.
- **Oracle:** `just completion-oracle` records dotty's answers in `tests/repl-completion/*.tab`
  on JDK 21; the files are committed and the tests compare against them.
- **Probe typing:** each Tab types the input in a throwaway wrapper object, as `:type` does,
  and the typer stops at the tree under the cursor. The universe keeps the wrapper's symbols;
  the cost is measured, and rollback is #70.
- **JDK members:** from a table generated by reflection on JDK 21, for the classes Scala
  Native's javalib has; a member is offered only if it is in the table and in javalib's TASTy.
- **Commands:** all 15 of dotty's commands complete, also those we do not implement.
- **Display:** labels without backticks, alphabetical; no types, no signature on an exact
  match.

## Consequences
- JDK-only names (`java.lang` nested classes, JDK packages, members javalib lacks) are misses.
- Completing a command we do not implement leads to its error message.
- The universe grows with each Tab until #70.
- A new JDK changes the oracle's answers; the `.tab` headers record the one they came from.
